The **Recent Calls** panel gives you a live snapshot of how your agent is performing, right inside the Advanced Editor. No need to jump to the Calls section to check if a change worked — just glance at the right panel.

<Info>
Recent Calls lives in the right panel of the Advanced Editor, alongside Meetzy, Playground, Tests, and Changes.
</Info>

## What it shows

The panel displays up to **20 calls from the last 30 days** for the agent you currently have open. Each entry includes:

- **Status badge** — the current state of the call (see below).
- **Success indicator** — a green checkmark when the call was marked as `success`.
- **From → To** — the phone numbers involved in the call.
- **Summary** — a short description of what happened, when available.
- **Relative time** — how long ago the call happened (e.g. "3 hours ago"). Hover over it to see the exact date and time.
- **Duration** — formatted as `mm:ss`.

<Info>
Only the most recent 30 days of activity are loaded, capped at 20 calls, to keep the panel fast and focused on what matters right now.
</Info>

Every call card gives you three ways to dig deeper, without ever leaving the Advanced Editor for the first two:

1. Click anywhere on the card to **listen to the recording with the transcript inline**.
2. Hover the card and click **Inspect** to open the full call detail.
3. Hover the card and click the arrow icon to **open that same call in the full Calls page**, with the filter already applied.

## Call statuses

| Status label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Finished** | The call completed normally. |
| **On call** | The call is currently in progress. |
| **Ringing** | The call is being dialed. |
| **Scheduled** | The call is queued or a recall has been scheduled. |
| **No answer** | The recipient didn't pick up. |
| **Busy** | The line was busy. |
| **Voicemail** | The call was routed to voicemail. |
| **Wrong phone** | The phone number format was invalid. |
| **Failed** | The call could not be completed due to an error. |

<Info>
Colors follow a simple convention: green for finished/successful calls, red for failures, amber for in-progress or scheduled calls, and gray for everything else.
</Info>

## Panel states

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Loading">
    On the very first load, the panel shows a skeleton placeholder while it fetches data. Subsequent refreshes update the list in place without blocking the UI.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Empty">
    If the agent hasn't received any calls in the last 30 days, you'll see a friendly empty state letting you know calls will show up here once they start coming in.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Error">
    If something goes wrong while fetching calls, an error message is shown along with a **Retry** button.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Populated">
    Calls are listed newest first, each as a clickable card with status, participants, summary, time, duration, and a row of hover actions.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Refreshing the list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the refresh button">
    In the panel header, click the circular arrows icon next to the call count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the spinner">
    The icon spins while the latest calls are being fetched. The rest of the interface remains fully usable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review updated results">
    The list refreshes in place — no page reload needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
You can also refresh with the keyboard shortcut <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>R</kbd> without moving your hands away from the editor.
</Info>

## Listening to a call and reading the transcript

Clicking anywhere on a call card — not just a dedicated button — opens the recording player **inline, as a modal on top of the editor**. This is the same player used on the full Calls page, so you get the audio controls and the full transcript side by side, without any page navigation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a call card">
    Click anywhere on the row, or hover the card and press the **Escuchar** button that appears in the actions row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the recording and transcript">
    The modal opens with the audio player and the complete transcript of the conversation, so you can confirm what was actually said on the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the modal">
    Close it to return to the Advanced Editor exactly where you left off — no tab switching, no lost context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
This replaces the previous behavior of opening the recording in a new browser tab. Everything now happens inline, keeping you focused on the agent you're editing.
</Info>

## Inspecting a call

Need more than the recording — status details, structured data, function calls, or metadata? Hover a call card and click **Inspect**.

<Info>
Inspect opens the same detailed call view used across Meetzy, as a modal inside the Advanced Editor. It's the fastest way to debug a specific interaction without losing your place in the editor.
</Info>

## Opening a call in the full Calls page

Sometimes you want the complete Calls experience — filters, export, call history — centered on one specific call. Hover a call card and click the arrow icon in the actions row.

This opens a **new tab** on the full Calls page with that call's recording filter already applied, so the page loads straight into the context of the call you clicked.

<Info>
Unlike **Escuchar** and **Inspect**, this action intentionally leaves the Advanced Editor and opens a new tab, since the full Calls page offers capabilities (filtering, exporting, browsing history) that don't make sense inside a compact side panel.
</Info>

## Viewing all calls

Sometimes 20 calls from the last month isn't enough. Click **View all** in the top-right corner of the panel to open the full Calls section, pre-filtered to show only calls handled by the current agent.

<Warning>
The **View all** button is disabled until an agent is loaded in the editor — for example, while creating a brand-new agent that hasn't been saved yet.
</Warning>

<Info>
**View all** filters the Calls page by agent, showing every call for that agent. Clicking a specific call card instead filters by that single recording — use whichever fits what you're trying to check.
</Info>

## Tips

- Use Recent Calls right after publishing a change to quickly confirm the agent is behaving as expected on real calls.
- Hover over the relative timestamp to see the exact date and time the call took place.
- If a call has no summary, it usually means the summary hasn't been generated yet or evaluation wasn't configured for the agent.
- Reach for **Inspect** when you need structured details beyond the audio and transcript, and reach for the arrow icon when you need the full power of the Calls page around a specific call.
