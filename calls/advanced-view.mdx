The **Advanced Calls View** (`/calls-v2`) is an improved version of the classic Calls page, built for teams that need deeper control over how call data is displayed, filtered, and audited. It introduces configurable and pinnable columns, saved views, smarter filtering, an opt-in live-refreshing table, at-a-glance summary metrics, and a lightweight review workflow directly inside the table.

<Info>
The Advanced Calls View is currently rolling out alongside the classic Calls page. You can switch between the two at any time using the banner at the top of the page.
</Info>

## Switching between views

When you open the Advanced Calls View for the first time, you'll see a migration banner at the top of the page confirming you're on the new experience.

- The banner reads **"You're viewing the new Calls page"** with a **Switch back to the classic view** link — click it to return to the original Calls page (`/calls-old`) at any time.
- Your filters, column layout, and saved views on the Advanced Calls View are stored independently, so switching back and forth won't affect either page.
- If you had previously customized columns on the classic Calls page, that configuration is **automatically migrated** into the Advanced Calls View the first time you visit it, so you don't lose your existing setup.

<Warning>
The Advanced Calls View is under active development. If something doesn't behave as expected, use the classic view as a fallback while we continue improving it.
</Warning>

## Summary cards

At the top of the page, four summary cards give you an instant read on the calls currently matching your filters, without needing to scan the full table:

- **Volume** — total number of calls matching the active filters.
- **Avg. latency** — the average AI response latency across those calls, so you can spot performance regressions at a glance.
- **Avg. duration** — the average call duration for the filtered set.
- **Reviewed** — how many of the filtered calls have already been marked as reviewed, so your team can track audit progress in real time.

<Info>
Summary cards always reflect your **current filters** — including the Suspicious calls filter — so they update instantly as you refine your search.
</Info>

## Configurable and pinnable columns

Unlike the classic Calls page, the Advanced Calls View lets you fully customize which columns appear in the table, in what order, and which ones stay anchored in place.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Columns panel">
    Click the **⚙️ Columns** button in the top-right corner of the page to open the column manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or hide columns">
    Toggle any column on or off. This includes standard fields (status, duration, agent, tags) as well as dynamic fields discovered automatically from your call inputs and outputs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder columns">
    Drag columns into the order that makes the most sense for your workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin important columns">
    Click the pin icon next to a column to anchor it. Pinned columns are grouped together and stuck to the right edge of the table — like **Reviewed** and **Actions** by default — so they stay visible no matter how many other columns you add or how far you scroll horizontally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Dynamic fields are detected automatically from the structured inputs and outputs captured during your calls, so new fields from your agents will show up as selectable columns without any manual setup. Dynamic columns are labeled with their source (input or output) so you always know where the value comes from.
</Info>

## Saved views

Once you've configured your columns, pins, and filters the way you like, you can save that configuration as a **view** and switch back to it whenever you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure your table">
    Set up your columns, pinning, filters, and sorting exactly how you want them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Views menu">
    Click **👁️ Views** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the current configuration">
    Type a name for your view in the input field and click **Save current**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a saved view">
    Open the Views menu at any time and click a saved view's name to instantly apply it. Use the **✕** icon next to a view to delete it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Saved views are stored per user, so each teammate can build their own set of views (e.g. "Suspicious calls this week", "My agent's calls") without affecting anyone else's setup.
</Info>

## Live updates

Live polling is **opt-in** — the table doesn't refresh in the background unless you turn it on, so it never surprises you mid-review.

- Toggle **Live** near the top of the table to start polling for new calls automatically.
- While active, a small live indicator confirms polling is running.
- Newly arrived calls that match your current filters are briefly highlighted in the table so they're easy to spot as they come in.
- Live polling respects your current filters, sort order, and scroll position — turning it on won't reset or interrupt what you're looking at.

<Info>
Live polling automatically pauses while you're interacting with elements like the review note editor or an open modal, so you never lose in-progress input. It resumes once you're done.
</Info>

## Filtering calls

The filter bar gives you several ways to narrow down the calls you're looking at.

### Date range

Choose from common presets (Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, This month, etc.) or define a fully custom start and end date and time.

<Info>
Unlike the classic Calls page, day-based presets here (such as "Today" or "Last 7 days") end at 23:59:59 of the last day, instead of rolling into 00:00 of the following day.
</Info>

### Agents

The agent filter uses a chip-based multi-select:

- Start typing an agent's name to search.
- Click an agent from the dropdown to add it as a chip.
- Remove a chip by clicking the **✕** on it, or clear all selected agents at once.
- Leaving the filter empty shows calls from all agents.

### Tags

Tags work the same way as agents — search, select, and manage them as chips. This makes it easy to combine multiple tags to narrow down a specific segment of calls.

### Status

Filter by one or more call statuses, including Open, Closed, No answer, Voicemail, Busy, Failed, Transferred, and Blocked.

### Suspicious calls

The **Suspicious calls** filter is a one-click way to surface calls that are worth a closer look. It's a client-side filter that flags a call as suspicious if **any** of the following are true:

- The AI response latency was unusually high.
- The call ended with a problematic status (`no-answer`, `failed`, or `blocked`).
- The call's goal was not met.
- The call has no transcript at all.

<Info>
This is intentionally an **OR** condition rather than an **AND** condition. Combining several individual filters (like status) would only show calls matching *all* conditions, which tends to under-match what auditors actually want to review first. The Suspicious calls filter is designed to catch anything that looks off, from any angle.
</Info>

### Free-text and Call ID search

Use the search box to look up calls by transcript content, or filter directly by a specific Call ID.

## Review and audit workflow

Each row in the table includes a **Reviewed** control that lets your team track which calls have already been checked, along with an optional note. This column is pinned to the right edge of the table by default, so it stays visible no matter how many columns you've added.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark a call as reviewed">
    Click the review indicator on a call's row to toggle its reviewed status. The **Reviewed** summary card at the top of the page updates immediately to reflect the new count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an audit note">
    Click the note icon to open a small inline editor and write context about why the call was flagged, resolved, or worth revisiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save automatically">
    Notes are saved automatically a moment after you stop typing — no need to click a separate save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Only one review note editor can be open at a time. Clicking outside the editor, or opening a note on another row, will close the current one.
</Info>

This review workflow is especially useful when combined with the **Suspicious calls** filter: turn on **Live** to catch new calls as they come in, flag the suspicious ones, review them one by one, leave a note explaining the outcome, and mark them as reviewed once resolved.

## Exporting to Excel

The Advanced Calls View supports two export modes:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Export all filtered calls">
    Click **Export to Excel** to export every call currently matching your active filters, respecting your selected columns.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Export selected rows only">
    Select one or more rows using the row checkboxes, then click **Export selection (N)**. This exports only the calls you've selected, and always includes the **Reviewed** status and the **audit note**, regardless of whether those columns are currently visible in the table.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
Selective export is ideal for sharing a curated set of reviewed or suspicious calls with a manager or client, without exposing your entire filtered dataset.
</Info>
